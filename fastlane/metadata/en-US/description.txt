Adhan is a beautiful prayer times app. It is 100% free, forever ad-free, and respects your privacy.

Features:
- Accurate Prayer Times: Real-time countdowns for Tahajjud, Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.
- Full Adhan Alarms (AlarmKit): Schedule loud, full-length adhan alarms that play even in Silent Mode (iOS 26+).
- 17 Adhan Sounds: High-fidelity recordings from muezzins worldwide. Customize the sound for each prayer.
- Tahajjud & Fajr Pre-Alarms: Gentle wake-up alerts 10 to 120 minutes before prayers.
- Custom Daily Alarms: Create unlimited custom alarms.
- Elegant Widgets & Live Activities: 6 Home and Lock Screen widget sizes, plus real-time Live Activities on your Dynamic Island.
- Qibla Finder: Smart compass with haptic feedback when aligned.
- Smart Calculations: 13 calculation methods with automatic location selection, Hanafi/Standard Asr rules, and high-latitude adjustments.

Fully localized in English, Arabic, Indonesian, and Turkish.
