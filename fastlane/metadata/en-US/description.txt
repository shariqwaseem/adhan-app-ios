Adhan is a prayer times app that stays out of your way. Free, no ads, and private: everything is calculated on your device, with no account or backend behind it.

Prayer times
Tahajjud, Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, with a live countdown to the next prayer. Your location is detected automatically and times update on their own when you travel, or pick any city by hand.

Alerts
For each prayer choose nothing, a notification, or a full alarm. Alarms play the complete adhan out loud even on silent or in a Focus. Add a second alert before or after the prayer, for example to wake ahead of Fajr, and create your own daily alarms.

Adhan recordings
Recordings of muezzins from around the world, with a different one for each prayer if you like. They download when selected, so the app stays small.

Widgets
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the next prayer or the whole day.

Qibla
A compass that points to the Qibla and taps gently when you are facing the right way.

Calculation
The method is chosen automatically from your location. You can also pick one yourself, set custom Fajr and Isha angles, choose Hanafi or Standard Asr, and adjust for high latitudes.

Siri
Ask Siri for the next prayer or the time of a specific one.

Languages
English, Arabic, Indonesian and Turkish, with full right-to-left support for Arabic.
